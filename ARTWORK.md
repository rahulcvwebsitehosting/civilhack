# Artwork and visual direction

The new cover illustration was generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool for this redesign. It is fan artwork inspired by Jujutsu Kaisen, not an official promotional image.

## Final prompt brief

Create a premium wide manga-ink hero illustration for a Jujutsu Kaisen themed event website. Satoru Gojo dominates the foreground with white hair, a dark high collar, and a domain-expansion hand seal; Ryomen Sukuna appears behind him with facial markings and his own hand seal. Place a Japanese shrine and huge crimson moon behind the characters. Keep the left side dark and quiet for live website headings. Use rich charcoal black, warm parchment, restrained crimson, and dusty violet shadows, with highly detailed ink linework and paper texture. Make the characters unmistakable and the composition cinematic. Avoid blue/cyan neon, cyberpunk grids, interface graphics, watermarks, or text baked into the image.

The landing-page picture selects the appropriate source, reserves its dimensions, and prioritizes loading. The original JJK track/domain images remain available and use restrained monochrome/sepia treatments. Genuine campus photography retains its original color.

Typography combines Permanent Marker for the cover title, Anton for display headings, Oswald for compact labels, DM Sans for body copy, and Noto Serif JP for Japanese details. Fonts load with `display=swap` and local fallbacks. Seals and the favicon are resolution-independent SVG/CSS.
