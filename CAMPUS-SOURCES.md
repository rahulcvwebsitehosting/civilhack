## AI enhancement — 2 October 2026

Built-in imagegen was used for all three edits. PNG masters and optimized WebP website versions are saved alongside the originals in `dist/assets/campus/`, named `printer-enhanced`, `products-enhanced` and `printing-work-enhanced`.

Prompt set: enhance clarity, natural exposure and concrete texture; retain the printer, existing products and lab composition; remove every person, body part, reflection and human shadow; reconstruct occluded backgrounds; do not add equipment or products. The printer image received one correction to leave the two top placards blank rather than show reconstructed lettering. The collage preserves four panels and shows empty concrete stools. WebP conversion only compresses the completed AI edits.
